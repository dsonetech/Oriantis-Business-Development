# Oriantis Business Development

PHP/MySQL quotation management application for Oriantis Business Development.

## Initial scope
- Bilingual interface: English / French
- Hotels and room purchase rates
- Services and service purchase rates
- Internal exchange rates
- Agencies / clients
- Quotes with per-pax costing
- Oriantis margin per pax
- Multi-currency selling prices

## Planned next steps
- PDF generation
- Email sending
- WhatsApp sharing
- Quote history and duplication
